How scoring works

Overall Ranking shows every team's best score on each task, and the best score on a task glows. A green dot marks a new personal best from the last 10 minutes; ▲ and ▼ show recent rank changes. The other tabs show each task's own ranking.

Task scores

Each task has its own metric. Only your team's best submission on a task counts. A short cooldown applies between submissions to the same task.

Overall ranking

On each task, scores are rescaled to 0–1 across all teams: the best team gets 1, the lowest 0. The overall score is the average over all tasks. Only teams with a score on every task are ranked.

nt = st − mintmaxt − mint
overall = 13 Σt nt

st is your team's best score on task t; mint and maxt are the lowest and highest best scores of all teams on that task. If every team has the same score on a task, each gets 0.5 on it.

Public and final scores

While the hackathon runs, the board shows public scores on about 30% of each task's evaluation data. When submissions close, everyone is ranked on the other 70%, which nobody has seen.
